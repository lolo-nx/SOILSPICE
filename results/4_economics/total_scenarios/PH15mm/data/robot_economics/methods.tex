All cleaning strategies are compared over a 25-year economic horizon at a 5\% discount rate. The annual costs and clean revenues are held constant at their simulated ten-year mean values, as in the existing model. Robot initial investment is 2.4--8.2 EUR/m$^2$, converted using the existing 4.6 m$^2$/kWp factor to 11.04--37.72 EUR/kWp. Robots are purchased at years 0, 12 and 24, with unchanged real purchase cost at each replacement. Maintenance and repair cost 5\% of initial investment annually, with year-end payments in years 1--25. The present value of these costs is divided by the 25-year annuity factor (14.09394456604475) to obtain an equivalent annual ownership cost. This is added once to the simulated residual production-loss cost in both with-band and no-band cases. Manual cleaning fees are not applied to the daily robot. The main case uses a linear remaining-life credit of 11/12 of the year-24 robot purchase cost at year 25, discounted to year zero. This is an assumed service-value credit, not an observed or guaranteed resale value. Zero residual credit is evaluated separately as a sensitivity. No residual credit is assigned to the robots exhausted at replacement. No new energy, water, escalation or degradation assumptions are introduced.
