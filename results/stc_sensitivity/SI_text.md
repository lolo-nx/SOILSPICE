Using the unchanged fitted cell-level circuits, we evaluated the relative module power loss at irradiances of 200–1000 W m−2 for 56 bottom-edge band configurations. Clean and shaded maximum powers were recomputed at the same irradiance and explicit operating temperature of 25 °C, with a global 0.02 V voltage sweep. The maximum absolute departure from the corresponding STC relative loss was 10.44 percentage points; 16 of 224 non-STC cases exceeded 2 percentage points. The largest exception was SunPower (IBC), triangle band, portrait, h=80 mm, tau_I=0, at 200 W m−2. For the 60 mm rectangular geometry at tau_I=0.44, all eight module/orientation configurations remained within 0.78 percentage points over the tested irradiances. A change in the MPP bypass-diode state occurred in 1 condition. The archived calculations were reproduced separately using their original nominal-temperature settings; their native operating temperature was 27 °C. Because the existing parameter t also sets diode TNOM, a physically consistent independent temperature sweep was not supported and was not imposed. These calculations assess numerical sensitivity within the existing model and do not independently validate either its low-irradiance response or the annual STC-loss approximation.
