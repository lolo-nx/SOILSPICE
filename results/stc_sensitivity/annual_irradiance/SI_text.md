We estimated the irradiance-only effect on annual additional band losses using the existing 2016–2025 hourly weather and simulated rain-driven band-transmittance trajectories for the PH15 mm scenario (rectangular band, h=60 mm, f=0.1, no manual cleaning). All positive clean-energy production was retained; no irradiance threshold was imposed. Each hour was weighted by its clean energy after the existing uniform-soiling attenuation. The correction L25(G,tau)−L25(1000,tau) from paired clean/shaded LTspice calculations was added to the original archived STC loss lookup, preserving the archived baseline and avoiding a nominal-temperature offset. Effective irradiance after uniform attenuation was used in the primary calculation. Median annual band losses over four modules and two orientations changed as follows: Almería: 3.768% to 3.795% (+0.026 percentage points); Doha: 6.979% to 6.940% (-0.040 percentage points); Neuchâtel: 0.136% to 0.140% (+0.004 percentage points). This is a conditional numerical estimate of the irradiance effect; the electrical loss correction is evaluated at 25 °C and does not assess the unvalidated electrical temperature dependence. Cleaning policies and economic results were not reoptimised.
